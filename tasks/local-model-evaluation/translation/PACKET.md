# EN -> PT-BR literary translation contract

Status: awaiting the owner-supplied English source and selected passage.

The candidate will receive the agreed source context and translate the defined
approximately 1,500–2,500-word range into PT-BR. Preserve names, paragraph and
dialogue structure, headings, and meaningful formatting.

Evaluation is blind to model identity and prioritizes semantic fidelity,
omission/addition control, literary voice/register, syntactic intelligence,
lexical choices, rhythm, idiomatic PT-BR, and continuity. Automated checks may
only verify structure, names, numbers, headings, obvious omissions, and format;
they are not a substitute for human literary judgment.
